# 1.0.4

*   **1.20's look and feel is now Backport Core's**, so it shows with any 1.20 or later backport, with or without this mod: the title logo with Minceraft and "Java Edition", the window icon, 1.20.x's splashes and wording, F3 + 1 and F3 + 2, Ctrl + scroll for GUI Scale, Menu Background Blur, 1.20.5's loading and dimension-change screens, the shield arm following your look and Ctrl + pick block without "(+NBT)". Your settings are kept. The cherry grove panorama, the music and the brushing pose stay in this mod.
*   The world loading screen's blur now matches the menus' behind it.

Needs Backport Core 1.4.7 or newer.

# 1.0.3

*   **Fixed: the chiseled bookshelf broke into black and purple particles.** Its model named its particle texture in a way 1.12.2 cannot read.

# 1.0.2

*   **Realistic Terrain Generation** (and RTG Unofficial): cherry groves now generate in RTG worlds, on RTG's forest hills. RTG turned them into plains. Needs Backport Core 1.4.5.
*   **With Game Drops Backport, 26.1's babies**: baby camels, snifflets, armadillos and every wolf variant's puppies have their own models, textures and animations, and a golden dandelion puts a snifflet back to the start of its 48000 ticks. They follow Game Drops' Baby Models setting.
*   **With Game Drops Backport, more of 26.x**: poplar hanging signs, this mod's cherry trees at the abandoned camps, fingerspit's music in the biomes as in 26.2, and 26.2's flat sign editing screen, for 1.12.2's oak signs and other mods' vanilla woods too (Game Drops supplies their textures).
*   **Boats are now Backport Core's**, one boat and one boat with chest for every backport's wood. Cherry boats, bamboo rafts and their chest forms look, float, steer and drop as before, and boats already in your worlds are kept, with their wood, the chest's contents and whoever rides them.
*   **Cherry and bamboo boats with chest work as 1.19's**: right-clicking one whose seat is taken opens the chest, and hoppers fill and empty it.
*   Trail ruins are laid out by Backport Core's shared modern jigsaw placer, block for block as before.
*   With Backport Core 1.4.5 or newer, the world backup, low disk space and chunk failure toasts are drawn by Core, beside the other backports' toasts. With Core 1.4.4 they are drawn as before.

Needs Backport Core 1.4.4 or newer.

# 1.0.1

*   **Torchflowers glow**: planted or potted, a torchflower gives off light level 10, between a soul torch and a redstone torch. Not in Minecraft; turn off **Glowing Torchflowers** on the Sniffer settings page for vanilla's unlit flowers.

# 1.0.0

First full release: all of 1.20 Trails & Tales and 1.20.5's armadillo and wolf armor on Minecraft 1.12.2. Since the betas:

*   **All 18 languages**: German, Spanish (5 variants), French, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Dutch, Polish, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Turkish, Ukrainian and Chinese (Simplified and Traditional).
*   **Camels can be ridden**: in the betas a saddled camel stood still under its rider.
*   **Suspicious sand and gravel stay put**: those that generate over air or water no longer fall before you dig them.
*   **Relic gives a comparator signal of 14** from a jukebox, as in 1.20.
*   **1.20's changes to things 1.12.2 already has**, each with its own setting on the Mechanics page:
    *   mobs hit only what they can reach and see: no hits through thin walls, or on a player riding a horse from below; endermen reach higher;
    *   shields block while looking straight up or down;
    *   the area kept loaded around the world spawn is 5 by 5 chunks, as in 1.20.5;
    *   beds, wool and carpets of any color can be dyed another color;
    *   Jump Boost works on slimes and magma cubes;
    *   eating chorus fruit resets fall distance;
    *   a named arrow keeps its name when shot and picked up;
    *   bookshelves power an enchanting table through grass, snow layers and other replaceable blocks;
    *   1.20's block sounds for jukeboxes, levers, repeaters, comparators, tripwire hooks, sponges and cobwebs;
    *   the 1.20.3 bat model and animations;
    *   hoppers under a full block pick up no items;
    *   an ender pearl plays a sound where it lands;
    *   the custom name of a non-mob entity shows when you look at it;
    *   the 1.20 game rules `enderPearlsVanishOnDeath`, `projectilesCanBreakBlocks`, `playersNetherPortalDefaultDelay`, `playersNetherPortalCreativeDelay` and `spawnChunkRadius`;
    *   no dust when landing on a barrier;
    *   the Wither effect's 1.20 color.

Needs Backport Core 1.4.0 or newer.
